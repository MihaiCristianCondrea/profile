About Me

Based in Bucharest, Romania, I'm a part-time Android developer at Digi România. My work centers on Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, modern Android architecture, Material Design, UI/UX, and building maintainable products that move from ideas to real releases.

Professional Experience

Working with Digi România gives me hands-on experience shipping and maintaining customer-facing Android products while continuing to grow through production development.

  • Part-time Android Developer, Digi România: Build and maintain Kotlin and Jetpack Compose experiences for customer-facing Android products.
  • Independent development: Design, build, publish, and maintain my own applications, reusable libraries, developer tools, and supporting services.

Projects & Engineering

  • App Toolkit for Android: A modular, reusable Android library and sample application covering shared foundations, feature screens, integrations, dependency injection, and Navigation 3.
  • Android Studio Tutorials: Educational Android projects supported by backend and administration tooling for lessons and dynamic content.
  • Smart Cleaner for Android: A storage-management utility with cleanup, analysis, privacy, automation, widgets, and Wear OS features.
  • Net Probe for Android: A network diagnostics toolkit for speed testing, host inspection, local-network discovery, and connection analysis.
  • Broader development: My work also includes Windows applications, web tooling, backend infrastructure, Kotlin Multiplatform experiments, Material Design tooling, and AI projects.

Public & Private Work

My portfolio is a mix of proprietary applications and selected public projects. Many of my consumer applications are closed-source, while I continue to publish reusable libraries, developer tools, experiments, and other projects on GitHub when public source makes sense for the project.

Community & Learning

I enjoy exchanging ideas around Jetpack Compose, Android architecture, design systems, and developer tooling. Continuous learning remains part of how I work, whether that means exploring new platform APIs, refining architecture, or building small experiments to understand a technology properly.

Personal Interests

UI/UX design, animation, Kotlin-first development, developer tools, and experimentation shape much of my personal work. I tend to focus on the boundary between engineering and design, where architecture, interaction, and visual consistency have to work together.
